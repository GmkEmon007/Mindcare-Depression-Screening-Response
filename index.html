About MindCare Study

MindCare is an academic undergraduate research project conducted at Varendra University (VU), under ethical clearance reference VU/ERC/2026/042.

The objective of this research is to investigate explainable, context-aware machine learning models for early detection of student distress, assessing the interaction between academic pressure, social support, and sleep patterns.
